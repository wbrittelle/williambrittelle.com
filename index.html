WILLIAM BRITTELLE

Info

William Brittelle is a Brooklyn-based GRAMMY-winning producer, GRAMMY-nominated composer and creator of Eternal September, a largescale alternate artistic realtiy. He currently serves as a Prizma residency artist at Brown University, the house producer at figureight records, and co-founder/co-artistic director of New Amsterdam Records. More info...
